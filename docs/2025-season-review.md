# 2025 Season Review — Who Scored, and What It Teaches Us

Researched Sept 2026. Exact point totals vary slightly by source/scoring format;
treat rankings as solid and totals as approximate (full PPR).

## Positional leaders

- **QB:** Josh Allen led the position again — the dual-threat archetype (big
  rushing floor + high TD volume) keeps winning. This is exactly what DK
  scoring rewards most.
- **RB:** Jonathan Taylor, McCaffrey, and Bijan Robinson led — all three are
  bell cows with 65%+ opportunity share and real receiving work. No committee
  back sniffed the top tier.
- **WR:** Puka Nacua, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Amon-Ra St. Brown — all three are
  massive-target-share (27%+) receivers, not deep-ball specialists. Volume >
  splash plays in PPR.
- **TE:** Trey McBride, Dallas Goedert, Kyle Pitts — McBride's edge was pure
  target volume, again confirming: at TE, pay for targets or pay nothing.

## Durable lessons for 2026 picks

1. **Every elite fantasy season is a volume story.** The top ~15 players at
   RB/WR all had elite opportunity metrics; none got there on efficiency alone.
2. **The RB1 overall is always a receiving back.** McCaffrey's edge is the
   catch volume (PPR + yardage + bonus paths). In DFS, pay-up RBs must catch
   passes.
3. **Dual-threat QB or bust at the top.** Pure pocket passers rarely crack the
   weekly top-3 QB scores that win GPPs.
4. **Year-over-year turnover is real.** Roughly half the top-12 at each
   position falls out the next season (injury, role change, regression). Never
   assume last year's stud carries over — check the current role first.
5. **Week-to-week, even studs bust ~25–30% of the time.** That's why cash
   lineups chase floors and GPPs chase correlated ceilings rather than trying
   to "pick the guys who will be good."
