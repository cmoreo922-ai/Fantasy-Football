# 2026 Player Movement & Scheme Fit

Researched Sept 2026 (pre–Week 1). **Verify current rosters/depth charts before
using in-season** — trades and cuts keep happening. Update this doc as roles
become clear.

## The framework: does the player fit the scheme?

When a player changes teams, his fantasy value = new **role** × new
**environment**, not his old stats:

1. What role does the new offense need him for? (X receiver vs slot, bell cow
   vs committee, field-stretcher vs chain-mover)
2. Does the play-caller's history support that role producing? (see
   docs/coaching-trends-2026.md — tree predicts usage)
3. Who competes for his touches? Target/carry pies are zero-sum.
4. QB quality change: a WR moving to a worse passer loses value even with more
   targets.
5. Expect a 2–6 week ramp for receivers learning a new offense (chemistry,
   route tree); RBs translate fastest.

## Preseason 2026 signals worth carrying into September

Preseason tells us about **roles**, not production. What mattered:

- First-team snap patterns: who ran with the 1s and for how long
  (e.g., Caleb Douglas playing every first-team snap in Miami's opener).
- Rookie WRs with early depth-chart traction: Malachi Fields, Caleb Douglas,
  Ja'Kobi Lane (see docs/rookies-2026.md).
- Injury flags out of camp: Christian Kirk (calf, missed significant camp
  time — soft-tissue risk early), Tank Dell (first padded practice since
  2024 — role uncertain, don't trust early).

### How to weigh preseason info generally
- Trust: first-team usage, two-minute personnel, red-zone reps, coach quotes
  about roles, who returns punts (role clarity).
- Ignore: preseason box scores vs backups, long TDs against third-stringers,
  camp "hype" without snap evidence.
- The signal decays fast — Weeks 1–2 real usage overrides everything preseason.
