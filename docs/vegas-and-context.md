# Vegas Lines, Weather, and Weekly Context

Vegas implied team totals are the single best free predictor of team scoring.
The betting market prices in injuries, matchups, pace, and weather faster and
better than any fantasy site.

## Implied team totals

Formula from the spread and game total:

```
favorite implied total  = (game total + spread magnitude) / 2
underdog implied total  = (game total − spread magnitude) / 2
```

Example: Total 48.5, Team A −3.5 → Team A implied 26, Team B implied 22.5.

How to use:

- **27+ implied points:** elite environment — players here get a boost
  regardless of individual matchup. Prime stack targets.
- **24–27:** good. **20–24:** neutral. **Under 20:** fade zone (only elite-
  volume players survive bad environments).
- **High total + tight spread (e.g., 51, −2.5):** shootout profile → game
  stacks, pass catchers both sides.
- **Big favorite (−7 or more):** positive script for the favorite's RB
  (second-half clock-killing carries) and DST; negative for the favorite's
  downfield passing late. Underdog pass catchers gain garbage-time volume.
- **Line movement is signal.** A total dropping 3 points means the market
  learned something (injury, weather). Re-check before lock.

## Weather (check Saturday and again Sunday morning)

- **Wind is the real killer — 15+ mph sustained** hurts deep passing and
  kicking; 20+ is a serious downgrade for QBs/WRs and a boost to run games.
- Rain/snow: modest downgrade to passing efficiency, slight boost to rushing
  volume; fumbles up. Extreme cold: totals drop.
- Domes and closed roofs: weatherproof — and dome games historically play
  slightly over on scoring. When in doubt, prefer the dome game.

## Injury workflow (the biggest weekly edge)

Practice reports Wed–Fri; final designations Friday; inactives **90 minutes
before kickoff**.

- **DNP Wed+Thu is serious; a Friday full practice usually means playing.**
  "Limited all week" for a soft-tissue injury (hamstring especially) = risk of
  a snap count or setback even if active.
- The value isn't just the injured player — it's the **beneficiary**:
  - Starting RB out → backup RB at $4,800 with a 15-touch role = best value
    profile in DFS.
  - Alpha WR out → targets redistribute; slot/WR2 get the biggest bump.
  - LT or multiple O-linemen out → downgrade the whole offense, upgrade the
    opposing DST.
  - Key CB/EDGE out on defense → upgrade opposing pass game.
- For Sunday-morning uncertainty: if a player is a true game-time decision,
  have a pivot ready in the same salary range, and prefer players in later
  games so you keep late-swap flexibility.

## Game script thinking

Ask: "how does this game most likely unfold, and who touches the ball in that
world?"

- Trailing teams pass (~70% of plays when down 10+ in the second half) →
  pass-catching RBs and slot WRs eat; early-down grinders vanish.
- Leading teams run → workhorse RB + DST correlation.
- A "bad" blowout matchup can still feed a WR1 garbage-time production —
  don't auto-fade volume on bad teams; DO fade TD-dependent players on them.

## Weekly timeline

| Day | What to do |
|---|---|
| Tue/Wed | Opening lines out; first look at slate, note high totals |
| Wed–Thu | Practice reports; identify injury beneficiaries early (before prices/ownership adjust) |
| Fri | Final injury designations; narrow player pool |
| Sat | Build lineups; check weather; lock in cash core |
| Sun AM | Inactives (90 min pre-kick), final weather, line moves; finalize |
| Sun 3:30+ | Late swap on breaking news |
