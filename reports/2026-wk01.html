Cash vs GPP — Why We Build Two Different Animals

Cash · beat half the field

  • ~125 points wins. You need the 55th percentile, not a miracle.
  • Built on floor: locked touches, big target shares, no injury tags.
  • Being different is a disadvantage — if the obvious play is great, everyone should have him, including us.
  • Pays a small, steady return. This is the engine that grows the bankroll.
Our 5 cash lineups project ~125–130 by design — that IS the target.

GPP · beat 99.99% of the field

  • ~236 avg wins the Milly (Week 1 historical low: ~194). No one projects there — winners correlate there.
  • A projection is an average. A 130-projected lineup hits 240 in the slice of outcomes where a stack's game explodes — QB, two receivers, and the bring-back spike together.
  • Built on ceiling + leverage: stacks, and 2–4 players under 10% owned so a win isn't split with the crowd.
  • Expect most GPP entries to lose. That's the price of the ticket, which is why entry sizing is fixed and small.
All 25 GPP lineups carry a QB stack; most carry a bring-back.
